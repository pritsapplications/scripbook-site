ScripBook, Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

ScripBook doesn't collect, transmit, or store any of your information. There aren't any accounts, no servers, and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. ScripBook has no backend. We don't operate a server that receives your data, and the app makes no network requests of its own. We have no way to see what you record in it.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter, expenses, income, categories, budgets, recurring bills, and your appearance settings, is written to your device's local app storage. It's readable only by ScripBook, it never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it, and it's removed when you uninstall the app.

Actions that involve other apps

A few features hand data to another app, but only when you tap the button that does so:

  • Exporting a backup writes a JSON file and opens your system's share sheet. You choose where it goes. We never see it, and the destination app's own privacy policy applies.
  • Importing a backup opens your system's file picker so you can choose a file. The app reads only the file you select.
  • Copying the AI summary places a plain-text summary of your spending on your clipboard so you can paste it into an assistant of your choosing. Nothing is sent anywhere by the app itself. Once you paste it somewhere, that service's policy governs it.
  • The "Support the developer" link opens Ko-fi in your browser. Ko-fi is a separate service with its own privacy policy.

Permissions

ScripBook requests no device permissions. It doesn't access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, health data, or bank accounts.

Tracking and advertising

There's none. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind. Nothing about your usage is measured or reported.

Children

ScripBook is safe for all ages in the sense that it collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13. Because no information is gathered, none can be gathered from a child.

Your control over your data

Your data is yours and it's local. You can delete individual entries in the app, export a full copy at any time, or remove everything by uninstalling. There's no account to close and no request to submit, because we hold nothing to delete.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of ScripBook adds anything that transmits data, cloud sync, for example, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here with a new date above.
