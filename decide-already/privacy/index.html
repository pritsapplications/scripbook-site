Decide Already

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Decide Already doesn't collect, send, or share any information about you. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no ads.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no backend and makes no network requests of its own. We have no way to see what you flip, spin, type, or pick.

What the app saves, and where

To make the app nicer to come back to, it remembers a few things on your device:

  • Your wheels and their options, bracket options, and team names
  • Your last choices, like dice sides, number range, and player counts
  • Your settings: dark mode, sounds, and read aloud

This is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. It never leaves your device, and it's deleted when you uninstall the app. Questions you type into Ask the ball aren't saved at all.

Device features the app uses

  • Motion sensor: used only to notice when you shake the phone to decide. The readings are handled on your device and never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Vibration: a small tap when a result lands.
  • Speech: if you turn on Read aloud, results are spoken by your phone's built-in text-to-speech voice. Only the result is spoken, nothing is stored.
  • Sound: short sound effects, if you turn Sounds on.

Decide Already doesn't ask for any permissions. It doesn't use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.

Tracking and advertising

There isn't any. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no third-party tracking code, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Children

Decide Already collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13. Because no information is gathered, none can be gathered from a child.

Your control

Everything the app keeps is on your device. You can clear your lists inside the app, or remove everything by uninstalling it. There's no account to close and nothing to request, because we don't hold anything.

Changes to this policy

If a future version ever adds something that sends data off your device, this policy will be updated before that version ships, with a new date at the top.
