Decide Already

Privacy Policy for Discord

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Decide Already's Discord commands (like /flip and /wheel) answer your command and forget it. Nothing is saved, logged, sold, or shared.

This page covers the Discord commands only. The Decide Already phone app works differently and has its own policy.

What the commands receive

Using a Decide Already command in Discord, Discord sends us what's needed to answer it:

  • The command and whatever you typed into it, like wheel options, a question for the ball, or the people for teams
  • Your Discord user ID, username, and display name, so the result can say who flipped or spun
  • IDs for the server, channel, or DM you used it in, which Discord includes with every command
  • For Draw straws, the draw's message, so it can tell who has already drawn

What we do with it

Only one thing: work out the result and post it back into that chat. Your command is handled in memory and discarded as soon as the reply is sent.

What we keep

Nothing. There's no database, no logs of your commands, and no analytics. The only record is the result message itself, which lives in Discord like any other message in that chat, where Discord's own privacy policy applies. Draw straws keeps track of who has drawn inside that same message, not anywhere of ours.

Who else is involved

The commands run on Cloudflare Workers. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes each request to deliver it and keep it secure. We don't sell or share anything with anyone, and there's no advertising or tracking.

Pings and permissions

Results can name people but never ping them. The commands don't read your messages, and they don't ask for any permissions in your server.

Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or older, depending on the country). We don't knowingly receive information from anyone younger, and we keep nothing from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the Discord commands ever start keeping anything, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
