Is it really random?

Yes. Every tool gives each option an equal chance, and the wheel and bottle always stop exactly on the result they announce.

How do I shake to decide?

Give the phone a quick, firm shake on the penny, dice, wheel, ball, bottle, number, teams, or yes or no screen.

How does the finger picker work?

Everyone puts a finger on the screen and holds still. After a short countdown, one finger is picked. Lift every finger to go again.

How do I save more than one wheel?

On the wheel screen, tap + New to add a wheel. Hold any wheel's name to rename or delete it.

Where did the sounds go?

Sounds are off until you turn them on from the home screen, and they follow your phone's silent switch.
